Our Story

The First Spark of Forever

  1. A Match Made by Destiny

    Two strangers, living their own lives, unaware that destiny was quietly writing a beautiful story for them. Through a matrimony connection, Nithiya and Sadhasivam’s paths crossed. What began as a search for a life partner was about to become the beginning of a lifetime together.

  2. Where Our Story BeganSrirangam

    Some first meetings are ordinary, but some become memories that last forever. Our first conversation happened at the beautiful and divine Srirangam Temple. Surrounded by blessings and a sense of peace, two strangers met for the first time, unknowingly taking their first step towards forever.

  3. Two Families, One Beautiful Yes

    Sometimes, a beautiful beginning needs nothing more than two families coming together with happy hearts. As both our families got to know each other, the decision was made. It was a heartfelt yes from both sides, bringing two families closer and giving our story a new beginning.

  4. From Conversations to Connection

    What started with a simple introduction slowly turned into endless text messages, long phone calls and little moments of getting to know each other. Every conversation brought us closer, every call made us more comfortable, and every passing day made us realise that we were becoming an important part of each other’s lives.

  5. A Promise for Forever9th August 2026

    And then came the day that made everything official! On 9th August 2026, surrounded by the love and blessings of our families, we exchanged rings and promised to walk through life together. Two hearts, two families and one beautiful promise — forever.

  6. Our Forever Begins

    From a matrimony match to a temple meeting, from strangers to friends, and from conversations to a lifetime commitment, our journey has been nothing short of beautiful.

    We may not know every chapter that lies ahead, but we know one thing for sure — we are ready to write them together.

    And this is only the beginning of our forever.

Sadhasivam & Nithiya
